# Prompt 
```
Classify the following email into one of these categories: [spam, important, personal].

Email: "Hey! Yeah, I got that invoice (#84930 or whatever). I can pay it by tomorrow, but wait... why are we paying this again? Thought you already settled it last week. Let me know before I send the money."
```

# Result
```
Category: important

Why?

Even though this email mentions money and invoices (which sometimes appear in spam), it is a real conversation about a business transaction.

    • It's not spam: Spam is usually automated junk mail trying to sell you something or scam you. This is a specific, contextual question about a real invoice (#84930). 
    • It's not personal: While the tone is casual ("Hey!", "or whatever"), the core subject is business—paying an invoice.                                                
    • It is important: This requires a prompt response. If the recipient doesn't reply, the sender might delay or withhold payment.
```
