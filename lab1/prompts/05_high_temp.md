# Prompt 
```
Classify the following email into one of these categories: [spam, important, personal].

Email: "Hey! Yeah, I got that invoice (#84930 or whatever). I can pay it by tomorrow, but wait... why are we paying this again? Thought you already settled it last week. Let me know before I send the money."
```

# Results

## First attempt
```
Category: important

Why?

Even though it mentions an invoice (which might sound like spam at first), the context of the email tells a different story:

 1. Real Human Conversation: It uses casual, conversational language ("Hey!", "wait... why are we paying this again?"). Spam is usually automated and tries to make you panic or click a link immediately.
 2. Actionable Business Matter: It involves real money and a specific invoice number (#84930).          
 3. Caution: The sender is pausing payment to double-check a detail. Missing or ignoring this email could lead to unpaid bills, delayed projects, or communication issues with a client/colleague.

Because it requires attention and involves real business operations, it belongs in the important category 
```
