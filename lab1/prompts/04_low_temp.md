# Prompt 
```
Classify the following email into one of these categories: [spam, important, personal].

Email: "Hey! Yeah, I got that invoice (#84930 or whatever). I can pay it by tomorrow, but wait... why are we paying this again? Thought you already settled it last week. Let me know before I send the money."
```

# Results

## First attempt
```
Category: important

Why?

Even though this email mentions money and invoices (which sometimes appear in spam), it is a real conversation about a business transaction between people.

Here is why it fits important:

 • It requires action: The sender is asking a direct question ("why are we paying this again?") and waiting for an answer ("Let me know before I send the money") before they make a payment.
 • It's not spam: Spam emails are usually automated, trying to sell you something, or trying to steal your information. This is a specific, contextual message about a real invoice (#84930).
 • It's not purely personal: While the tone is casual ("Hey!"), the main topic is business and finances, making it a professional/important matter rather than just catching up with a friend.
```
